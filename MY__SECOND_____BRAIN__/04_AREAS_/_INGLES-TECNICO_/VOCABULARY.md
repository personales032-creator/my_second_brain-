# VERBS FOLLOWED ONLY BY **(TO + BASE VERB)**    
It means I must put the verb after **"TO"** , It always necesary all the time I shouldn´t use the **"ING"**  only **"TO"** remenber it .

- **DECIDE** : "We decided to migrate the database to PostgreSQL."
- **NEED** : "I need to refactor this controller before merging."
- **PLAN**: "They plan to deploy the new microservice on Friday."
- **EXPECT**: "We expect to finish the sprint tickets by tomorrow."
- **MANAGE**: "I finally managed to fix the memory leak."   (lograr hacer algo difícil) 
- **FAIL** :  "The container failed to start because the port was already in use."
- **AGREE** : "The client **agreed to extend** the deadline by two days."
- **OFFER** : "I offered to help the junior dev debug his SQL query."
- **REFUSE** : "The firewall refused to accept the incoming connection."
- **TEND** : "Unindexed queries tend to slow down the entire system."

# PHRASAL VERBS

Take notes in your mind about this excercise you need use it as much as you can because we need a native english in my pocket .

- **GET ALONG WITH OR GET ON WITH**: I get along with my friends  (llevarse bien con alguien)

- **GET OVER**: I have been trying to be strong to get over the death of my father  (superar una perdida , enfermedad , problema o ruptura )  (este phrasal verb necesita un objeto directo después: **"get over something"**) (get over the death of my father). No se usa en voz pasiva como "I get over for. ) 

- **TAKE CARE OF**:  I wish I could go out partying, but I have to take care of my sister. (cuidar de alguien o algo)

- **LOOK FORWARD TO**:  I'm looking forward to my monitor arriving at my house (esperar algo con ilusión ) , **Subject + look forward to + (sustantivo] OR (verbo con -ing)** 

- **GIVE UP**: I can´t do it , I give up (rendirse o dejar un habito)

- **PUT OFF**: I put off the interview because I don't feel so good. (posponer o retrasar)

- **FIND OUT**: I found out how to make a solar panel (averiguar o descubrir)

- **TAKE OFF**: before coming to my house , take off your jacket (quitarse una prenda )

- **COME UP WITH**: She **came up with** a great idea for the project. (idear,inventar o  proponer)
